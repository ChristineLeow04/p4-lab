remained unchanged over five seconds.

This indicates that the packet did not continue circulating
indefinitely.

forwarding actions. The next switch should drop the packet
when the hop limit is zero.

### Additional C4 Verification — Hop-Limit Drop

I repeated the tunnel-loop experiment using the updated P4 program and sent a packet with an initial hop limit of 4.

The s1 switch log showed that the packet matched tunnel destination ID 3 in `MyIngress.myTunnel_exact` and executed `MyIngress.myTunnel_forward`.

The action then called `mark_to_drop(standard_metadata)`, and the packet was dropped at the end of ingress.

This provides stronger evidence that the updated forwarding action terminates the loop when the tunnel hop limit is exhausted.

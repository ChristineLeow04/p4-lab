Observation:
After adding the tunnel hop limit, both switch log counts
remained unchanged over five seconds.

This indicates that the packet did not continue circulating
indefinitely.

With an initial hop limit of 4, the design allows four
forwarding actions. The next switch should drop the packet
when the hop limit is zero.

The totals in the log files include other traffic, so they
do not independently establish the exact packet pass count.
